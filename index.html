The basics and the rules

A POC is a project.

An objective, a KPI, a timeline and a decision. These are the sources behind the first part of the session.

The NASA scale

Technology Readiness Levels

Nine levels. Level 3 is called “proof of concept”, and each level has exit criteria written in advance.

  • NASA ESTO: the TRL table

Technical proof or market proof

Invention risk versus market risk

Does the technology work, or will someone pay for it? Two different questions.

  • Steve Blank, 2009

Rule 1

Tackle the monkey first

Test the hardest part first. The pedestal can wait.

  • Astro Teller, X blog, 2016

Rule 2

Decide before you start

Write the go, pivot and stop thresholds before the first experiment. The more you have spent, the harder it is to stop: this is called escalation of commitment.

  • Annie Duke, “Knowing When to Quit” (video)

One more tool

The eight DARPA questions

What DARPA asks before funding a programme. The last three are about cost, time and the “exams” that check for success.

  • The Heilmeier Catechism

The cases

Four cases, read with the run sheet.

Three real stories and one trap.

Case 1 · Alphabet X · stopped

Foghorn: fuel from seawater

The technology worked. The cost threshold was out of reach, so the team stopped.

  • X: the Foghorn project
  • Kathy Hannun, “Three things I learned from turning seawater into fuel”

Case 2 · SpaceX · success

Falcon 1: orbit at the fourth attempt

Three failures, then orbit in September 2008 with the last money available.

  • Physics Today, 2008
  • Falcon 1 launch history, Wikipedia
  • NASA, COTS programme lessons learned (PDF)

Case 3 · Intellia and Regeneron · success

NTLA-2001: CRISPR inside a patient

Six patients, one KPI. The TTR protein dropped by 52 % and 87 % at the two doses.

  • Gillmore et al., New England Journal of Medicine, 2021

Case 4 · Artificial intelligence · a trap

99 % accuracy, 0 % sensitivity

A fictional example, but a real trap: when a disease is rare, accuracy says very little. Choose the KPI before the POC.

  • Andrew Ng, from proof of concept to production (video)
  • Reuters (via Euronews), 2018: Amazon scraps a biased recruiting model
